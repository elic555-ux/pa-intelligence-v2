# Rehabilitation quantity pricing and scenario snapshots — 8.25.1

Section 07 now retains findings, a current-property source-photo reference, user-declared observation confidence, scope, quantity/unit, low/high unit prices, and price provenance. A row may use its legacy manually entered total or quantity × unit-price range. The high end is explicitly the planning amount; reserve applies once to the priced subtotal and range. No measurements, condition assessments or cost values are inferred from photographs. Manual assumptions remain distinct from named contractor quotes and versioned price books. Named documentary bases require source, valid calendar date and reference/version.

Version-one plans retain totals, explicit zero, exclusions, scopes and reserve. Incomplete amounts stay missing. Pricing completion is separate from review readiness: each active item must have a reviewed condition plus finding or work scope; the plan must be saved and have a rental or flip purpose. User-selected exclusions are explicit and retain their prior data. Total and row bounds prevent overflow. Local drafts retain invalid raw values across reloads and do not cross property identities. Conflicting newer cloud plans reject overwrite before writing. Existing owner authorization and database schemas are unchanged.

Section 08 offers separate explicit copy actions for rental and flip. They reject foreign, partial, unreviewed, mismatched-purpose or dirty plans. A copy uses the saved high total including reserve and records the range and canonical budget revision with its timestamp. Later budget changes show a stale warning without changing the scenario. The warning is also included in the section 01 overview. Editing a scenario amount detaches its old budget source, while document presets clear prior links. Scenario persistence continues to use its existing explicit fields and autosave rules. Source evidence and provider estimates are not replaced.

The revision key is a canonical JSON snapshot, not an authentication token or verification claim. It retains the actual entered scope and price metadata for identifying the copied version. Photos come only from current-property media already accepted by the report. Original-photo links open their matching image; unavailable references remain visibly unavailable. There is no new provider dispatch, scraper, schema migration, subscription, scheduled task or neighborhood cache in this change. PDF design remains deferred; export lifecycle functions are unchanged.
